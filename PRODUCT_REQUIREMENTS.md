# Product boundary

The Diagnostic Information System owns modality-neutral diagnostic orders, work lifecycle, canonical results, reports, provenance, audit, controlled distribution boundaries, and interoperability adapters. EMR/EHR/HIS, PACS/VNA, LIS, billing, identity providers, national exchanges, and patient applications remain external systems.

Step 1 deliberately implements no modality, clinical terminology content, medical thresholds, external endpoint, or real device protocol.
